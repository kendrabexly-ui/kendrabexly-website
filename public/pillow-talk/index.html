Frequently Asked Questions

A little clarity makes planning our time together feel simple, private, and effortless.

How do I request time with you?

Complete The Rendezvous form with your preferred date, start time, duration, location, and a little about what you have in mind. I personally review every request and will follow up if I would like to move forward.

Is screening required?

Yes. Valid identification is required before final approval. If your request moves forward, the screening instructions will be shared with you privately.

When is my date confirmed?

Your date is confirmed after I approve your request and receive the required 25% deposit. You will receive a confirmation once your date and time have been officially reserved.

When will I receive the exact location?

The exact address for an approved and confirmed date is shared privately two hours before our scheduled start time.

Can I make a same-day request?

Yes, when availability allows. Same-day requests must be submitted at least two hours before the requested start time to allow time for screening.
