A LITTLE PILLOW TALK

Questions, answered discreetly.

Everything you may want to know before making a private request.

How do I request time with you?

Complete The Rendezvous form with your preferred date, time, duration, and general plans. I review every request personally.

Is screening required?

Yes. Screening is required before final approval. If your request moves forward, instructions will be provided privately.

When is my date confirmed?

Your date is confirmed only after final approval and the required 25% deposit are complete.

When will I receive the exact location?

The exact address for an approved date is shared privately two hours before our scheduled time.

Can I make a same-day request?

Same-day requests must be submitted at least one hour before the requested start time and remain subject to availability.

Can we plan travel or extended time?

Yes. Choose Travel or Extended Time on the request form and share what you have in mind.
