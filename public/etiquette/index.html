Etiquette & Policies

The best experiences feel effortless because the expectations are clear from the beginning. A little consideration, discretion, and thoughtful planning allow us both to relax and enjoy our time together.

01

Respect & Discretion

A thoughtful introduction sets the tone for everything that follows. Please communicate kindly, honor my privacy, and treat both me and our time with genuine respect from the first message onward.

02

Screening

Safety and discretion protect us both. Valid identification is required before any date receives final approval. If your request moves forward, the screening instructions will be shared with you privately.

03

Reservations

Once your request has been approved, a 25% deposit secures your date and time. Until the deposit is received and your confirmation is complete, the requested time remains available.

04

Thoughtful Planning

Please arrive freshly showered, punctual, and ready to be present with me. If your plans change, communicate with me as early as possible so we can handle the adjustment thoughtfully.
