FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little clarity

How do I request time with you?

Complete The Rendezvous form with your preferred date, start time, duration, and location. I personally review every request and will follow up privately if I would like to move forward.

Is screening required?

Yes. Valid identification is required before final approval. If your request moves forward, screening instructions and any additional information needed will be shared with you privately.

When is the deposit requested?

The invitation to continue includes the private screening and deposit selection page. Payment is confirmed separately, and your date is not final until I send approval.

When is my date confirmed?

Your date is confirmed after screening is complete, the required 25% deposit is received, and I give final approval. You will receive a confirmation once your date and time are officially reserved.

When will I receive the exact location?

The exact address for an approved and confirmed date is shared privately two hours before our scheduled start time.

Can I make a same-day request?

Yes, when availability allows. Same-day requests must be submitted at least two hours before the requested start time to allow time for screening.
